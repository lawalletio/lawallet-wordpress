Built for real stores

Everything you need to get paid in Bitcoin

A complete Lightning toolkit for WordPress — checkout, payment verification (LUD-21, NIP-57, or NWC), and identity.

Lightning checkout

A WooCommerce gateway that invoices your Lightning Address via LNURL-pay. Fiat totals convert to sats automatically.

Server-verified payments

Orders complete only after settlement is verified server-side — via LUD-21 or NIP-57 zap receipts.

Lightning Address & NIP-05

Serve /.well-known/ discovery and route it to a LaWallet gateway.

Works with any address (NWC)

No LUD-21 or NIP-57? A Nostr Wallet Connect proxy receives the payment, confirms it, and forwards it to your Lightning Address — disposable wallets are created on demand.

Non-custodial

Never holds funds or keys. Payments settle to your wallet.

Open source

GPL-3.0, built on open standards: LNURL, LUD-16/21, NIP-05/57.
